# IProFixer Arabic Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary is the mandatory source of truth for Arabic public copy. The approved style is professional GCC business Arabic for hospitality, procurement, operations and food-and-beverage teams. Translation must communicate the intended business meaning rather than mirror English sentence structure.

## Voice

- Modern Standard Arabic with natural GCC business usage.
- Clear, confident and factual; no inflated claims.
- Address the reader directly where an action is required.
- Prefer specific nouns such as أدوات المائدة and أواني التقديم over the generic الأصول in customer-facing copy.
- Use the Arabic semicolon (؛), Arabic comma (،) and correct RTL arrow direction.

## Prohibited literal phrasing

Do not publish these expressions:

- القطع المجوفة
- الأصول المتعبة
- مسار العناية
- نافذة التشغيل
- نافذة التسليم
- إطلاق الجودة
- واقع التشغيل
- أصول العرض
- السعر العام

Use the approved contextual alternatives in this glossary.

## Sources and rationale

Terminology follows normal Arabic hospitality and procurement usage, with asset-management language reserved for governance contexts. ISO 55000:2024 is used as the conceptual reference for lifecycle and asset-management distinctions. UAE government procurement terminology is used as a regional reference for المشتريات، خدمات الضيافة، أدوات المطبخ، and خدمات الحفلات والمناسبات. Arabic lexical checks use the Cairo Arabic Language Academy dictionaries.
